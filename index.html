Study Design

6-Phase Double-Blind
Crossover Protocol

This is a 26-week N=1 crossover study. Each participant serves as their own control. The double-blind design means neither participant nor the DrRuby data team knows which phase contains the active supplement — eliminating placebo effect and observer bias simultaneously.

Phase 1
Week 0 – 2
Baseline Establishment
Dermoscope skin scan baseline · Lab panel · Full supplement audit · Lifestyle and hormone questionnaire. No intervention.
Phase 2
Week 2 – 6
Washout Period
Participant pauses all current supplements. Weekly scans continue to stabilize baseline signal and clear prior confounders.
Phase 3
Week 6 – 14
Blinded Intervention A
Participant receives either active supplement stack or identical placebo — double-blind. Daily dermoscope scan + weekly self-report.
Phase 4
Week 14 – 16
Second Washout
Crossover preparation. Scans continue to capture return to baseline before introducing the alternate condition.
Phase 5
Week 16 – 24
Blinded Intervention B
Crossover: participants who had active now receive placebo, and vice versa. Same scan and self-report cadence as Phase 3.
Phase 6
Week 24 – 26
Unblinding + Debrief
Results revealed. Personalized N=1 report delivered. Optional debrief session with Dr. Brenner's team included.

Participation Requirements

Who we're looking for

✓  Eligibility Criteria
  • ✓Women ages 30–60
  • ✓Perimenopausal or postmenopausal preferred
  • ✓No active autoimmune flare or cancer treatment
  • ✓Willing to pause current supplements for washout phases
  • ✓Smartphone with camera (dermoscope provided by DrRuby)
  • ✓Able to commit to 26 weeks
  • ✓English-speaking
  • ✓Not currently enrolled in another clinical study
  • ✓Willing to provide written informed consent
✗  Exclusion Criteria
  • ✗Currently pregnant or breastfeeding
  • ✗Active chemotherapy or radiation treatment
  • ✗Severe kidney or liver disease
  • ✗Known allergy to NR, NMN, or core study ingredients
  • ✗Active dermatological condition affecting scan validity
  • ✗Unable to complete weekly digital check-ins
  • ✗Currently using HRT (unless stable for 6+ months)
  • ✗Unwilling to provide written informed consent
Informed Consent

What you're agreeing to

01
Data Ownership
Your data belongs to you. DrRuby holds a limited, time-bound research license for anonymized aggregate analysis only. You retain full ownership and can request deletion at any time.
02
Right to Withdraw
You may withdraw from the study at any time, for any reason, without penalty. Data collected prior to your withdrawal request will not be used in any analysis after that point.
03
Blinding Protocol
Neither you nor the DrRuby data team will know which phase contains the active supplement until the formal unblinding at Phase 6. This eliminates placebo bias from both sides.
04
Not Medical Care
Participation does not constitute medical advice or treatment. Always consult your physician before altering any health regimen. DrRuby is a research and wellness platform, not a clinical provider.
05
Privacy & Security
All scan data is processed on-device. Identifiable data is never sold or shared with third parties. Study data is anonymized before any aggregate analysis by the research team.
06
Publication Rights
Anonymized aggregate findings may be submitted for peer review or shared publicly. Individual participant data will never be published or identifiable in any research output.
07
Supplement Safety
All supplements used in this study have been reviewed by Dr. Brenner's team. Participants are advised to disclose all current medications to their physician before enrollment.
08
IRB Status
This study is conducted under DrRuby's N=1 wellness research framework. IRB review status will be disclosed to accepted participants prior to signing formal consent documentation.
